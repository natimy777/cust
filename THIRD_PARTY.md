## Wallpapers and avatars

`assets/backgrounds/*/default-*.jpg` and `assets/avatars/*.png` are original images
generated procedurally for this repository and released under CC0 (public domain).

## Installed at setup time (not bundled)

powerlevel10k (MIT, cloned from GitHub), lazy.nvim (Apache-2.0, cloned on first
Neovim start), wlogout and the optional icon/GTK themes (AUR, built by you after
reading the PKGBUILD).
